Mossgate Idle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Mossgate Idle does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Game data

Your game progress (skills, items, settings, save slots and backups) is saved only on your own device, inside the app. We never receive it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your saves are deleted. You can use the in-game Export option to keep a backup copy yourself.

Internet use

The game runs offline. The only internet request it makes is to load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Like any web font request, this lets Google see your device's IP address. We do not control Google's handling of that request; see Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
